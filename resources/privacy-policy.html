1. Scope and Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Flying Vee Studios ("Flying Vee," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit claimingcosmos.com, play Claiming Cosmos, create an account, purchase a membership, contact support, or otherwise use our related services (collectively, the "Service").

This Policy applies to the Service operated by Flying Vee. Third-party websites, platforms, payment services, and advertisements have their own privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information, such as your email address, display name, account or player identifier, login information, age-related eligibility information, and authentication details.
  • Gameplay and competitive information, such as matches, actions, teams, rankings, achievements, progression, chat or communications, reports, penalties, and moderation history.
  • Technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, language, approximate location derived from IP address, referring pages, timestamps, session identifiers, crash data, and server or security logs.
  • Payment and membership information, such as purchase status, subscription tier, transaction identifier, billing interval, and payment status. Payment card details are normally collected directly by our payment processor rather than stored by Flying Vee.
  • Support and user-submitted information, such as messages, bug reports, appeals, screenshots, and other material you choose to send us.
  • Cookie, advertising, and analytics information, including cookies, web beacons, pixels, local storage, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and information about visits, ad views, clicks, and interactions.

We receive information directly from you, automatically from your browser or device, from gameplay systems, and from service providers such as authentication, hosting, payment, analytics, security, and advertising partners.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide accounts, matches, memberships, customer support, and other Service features.
  • Operate matchmaking, rankings, statistics, achievements, and game progression.
  • Detect cheating, bots, exploits, fraud, account sharing, ban evasion, attacks, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Moderate usernames, communications, reports, and player conduct, including through automated screening and human review.
  • Process purchases, maintain billing records, and administer subscriptions.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, measure, and improve the Service.
  • Display, measure, and where permitted personalize advertisements.
  • Communicate about accounts, support requests, security, material policy changes, and Service updates.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements, resolve disputes, and protect players, Flying Vee, and others.

4. Public Player Information

Claiming Cosmos is a competitive multiplayer game. Display names, player identifiers, match history, rankings, statistics, achievements, team information, and similar competitive data may be visible to other players or the public. We may also show recent display names or an account restriction indicator when reasonably useful for moderation, transparency, or preventing impersonation. Do not use a display name that reveals information you do not want made public.

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Service working, remember settings, protect accounts, measure traffic and performance, prevent fraud, and support advertising. Some are placed by Flying Vee, while others are placed or read by third parties.

We expect to use Google advertising services, such as Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may place or read cookies, use web beacons, collect IP addresses or other identifiers, and receive information about your browser, device, visits, ad impressions, clicks, and interactions. Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, limit repeated ads, detect invalid traffic or abuse, and, when permitted and selected, personalize advertising based on interests or activity across sites and apps.

Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or identifiers for functions such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention. Where required by law, we will request consent before activating nonessential advertising or analytics technologies and will offer a way to change your choices.

Learn more about Google's practices at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and manage Google advertising choices through My Ad Center.

You may also control cookies through your browser. Blocking certain cookies may affect login, preferences, measurements, or other Service functions.

  • Service providers that perform hosting, cloud storage, authentication, payment processing, analytics, customer support, email, security, anti-cheat, and technical operations for us.
  • Google and other advertising technology providers to deliver, personalize where permitted, measure, and secure advertisements.
  • Other players and the public for the competitive information described in Section 4.
  • Authorities, courts, advisers, or affected parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or enforce our agreements.
  • A buyer, successor, or adviser in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction.

Where applicable, you may opt out using our cookie or privacy controls, a recognized browser-based opt-out signal such as Global Privacy Control, or by contacting us.
