Skull Island

Skull Island in Marauder's Sea is the death's-head archipelago. Eye-socket lagoons, jaw islands, folklore, and how to play.

Folklore

Sailors swear the sea carved a warning. The two round bays are the eyes; the islets between them are the nose; the crescent to the south is a grin that never closes. Superstition says you toss a coin into the left lagoon before you beach a boat. The right lagoon is for debts you do not want paid.

How to play it

Fight for the eye sockets — they are sheltered water with beaches. Rivers that spill into those lagoons let you sneak inland. The jaw island is a flank: ignore it and someone will wrap your navy from the south.
