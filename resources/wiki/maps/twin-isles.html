Twin Isles

Twin Isles is a two-island map in Marauder's Sea, the free pirate strategy game. Folklore, layout, and how to play the strait.

Folklore

Chart-makers say the twins were once one mountain that a jealous current split overnight. Each isle still faces the other like a rival captain across a tavern table. Crews who take both shores in the same night are said to sail with doubled luck — and doubled grudges.

How to play it

Treat the central channel as the prize. Spawn on one bulk, push a Port on the inner beach, and contest the stepping-stone islets before the other side walls the strait with Port Guns. Rivers that dump at beach deltas are boat highways into the interior.
