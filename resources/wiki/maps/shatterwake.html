Shatterwake

Shatterwake original art: a cracked world with void channels cut through the crust.

Folklore

The wake is what was left after the crust failed. Water still pools in the green bowls. The black cuts are streets now.

How to play it

Use the channels. A Starport on the rim can send hulls into the interior if the rift stays open. Hold high crust for Cities while the void fight happens in the cuts.
