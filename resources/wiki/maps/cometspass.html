Comet's Pass

Comet's Pass original art: a shattered system with a bright trail ships can run between worlds.

Folklore

A comet did not politely pass. It cut a road. The glowing wake is still the fastest line between the broken globes, and every captain who uses it pretends they were first.

How to play it

The trail is the prize. Starports on the rims feed Voidships into the pass. Lakes on the green shards still want Harbors. Do not empty a world to hold the wake — someone will land behind you.
