Harbor

Harbors in Claiming Cosmos spawn Warships, Marauders, and Tenders on planet lakes and run trade ships for gold.

Folklore

A Harbor is a mouth on wet ground. Feed it and it spits hulls into the lakes. Starports own the void; Harbors own the blue.

In the game

Build near lake or ocean water on a planet. Spawns Warships, Marauders, and Tenders. Auto trade between harbors for gold unless trade is stopped. Ships can destroy a Harbor; it does not heal. Cost shares a scaling bucket with Factories.
