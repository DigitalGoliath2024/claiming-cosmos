Armory

The Armory in Marauder's Sea is unique: one per player. Each upgrade is a stronger weapon age, then mines at level 4.

Folklore

One shed per captain. That is the rule. Every time you advance the Armory, you get stronger — ballistic, electromagnetic, nuclear, energy, then plasma.

Ledger

Level 0 — Ballistic
Gunpowder firearms, cannons, conventional explosives. No Armory yet.
Level 1 — Electromagnetic
Railguns, coilguns, mass-driver style weapons.
Level 2 — Nuclear
Fission and fusion-scale ordnance.
Level 3 — Energy
Lasers, particle beams, directed-energy weapons.
Level 4 — Plasma
Advanced plasma weapons. Unlocks naval mines.

In the game

One per player. Unique. Each upgrade is a stronger weapon age. Level 4 unlocks Naval Mines. Costs $500k / $1.5M / $3M then $2M. Upgradable. About 8 seconds to build.
