# Claiming Cosmos 0.1.0

This is **Claiming Cosmos** — a whole-system war. Your planet is only the start. Harbor fleets fight on lakes and seas. Starport fleets fight in the void between worlds.

## Two fleets

- **Harbor** — Sits on owned land next to a planet lake or sea. Spawns Warships, Marauders, Tenders, and lake transports. Trade stays on that water.
- **Starport** — Sits on the planet rim facing the void. Spawns Voidships, Corsairs, Vestals, Lancers, and Landers. Trade runs between Starports across the black.
- **Anti-ship battery** — Place on a lake shore or the planet rim. Fires on ships. Upgrade to level 10. Repairman heals from level 4.
- **Planetary Battery** — Land artillery on owned ground. Auto or manual aim. Scorches enemy land; water and void landings are duds.
- **Mines** — Place on water or void. Unlock at Armory 4.

## Hulls

- **Warship / Marauder / Tender** — Harbor capital, raider, and unarmed repair hull.
- **Voidship / Corsair / Vestal / Lancer** — Starport capital, raider, repair hull, and laser lock ship.
- **Lander** — Troop drop across the void between planets.

## Armory

One Armory. Each upgrade is a stronger weapon age: Ballistic → Electromagnetic → Nuclear → Energy → Plasma. Level 4 also unlocks mines.

## Maps

Public games use cosmic maps: **Sol**, **Collision**, **Verners System**, **Comet's Pass**, **Shatterwake**, **Four for War**, **Event Horizon**, **One Big World**, **The Hollow World**, **Sol System**, **Shattered**, **Mars**, **Luna**, **Pluto**, **Titan**, and **Milky Way**.
